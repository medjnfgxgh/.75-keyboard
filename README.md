# .75-keyboard
An 83 key mechanical keyboard with a rotary encoder for volume and brightness. This was designed to be similar to a 75% keyboard.

### Why?
I was complaining to my friend about how terrible the keyboards in the computer labs at school are, and he suggested I bring my own. One problem, I don't have a spare keyboard. "Just make one", he said, "How hard could it be?". This was very clearly a joke but it did give me an idea for my next project, why not make my very own mechanical keyboard. It would test my CAD and PCB design skills, as well as my soldering, 3d printing and programming knowledge. After a little bit of research, I landed on my design with 83 keys and a rotary encoder.
